# v0.4.0 — Welcome to the farm

Three pixel pals for your Mac: Maple, Mochi, and Clover.

- Six-frame walking and hopping animations, with a relaxed playback speed.
- Curled sleeping poses, blinking, a typing keyboard, and animated headphones.
- Optional virtual-pet care: food, cuddles, play, and rest.
- Three sizes, saved per-pet needs and position, and transparent click-through.
- No hover reactions, accounts, ads, or network requests.

Apple Silicon macOS build. Requires macOS 14.2+. Locally signed, not notarized.
Audio detection observes active streams, so silent video is not detected and some paused players may keep headphones visible.
